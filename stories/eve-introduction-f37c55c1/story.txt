Let the story begin ************************************************* Prologue The sky drew closer to the earth as she sang of her misery, they darkened and thundered to the rhythm of her cries as she laid unmoving and drenched in mud. All she could think about or ponder on as she sorrowed away her last breath was the heavy betrayal that haunted her dreams up to this very god-forsaken moment.

"There is nothing more to live for" she concluded, praying that the rain will swallow her and make her one with the earth- but the wind held a different tale, the gods whispered into the night and the world was about to be plunged into something new. “She has served us right and doesn’t mock us even in her dying moment, her heart has been severely injured and now that she weeps with such grief, this world will know no peace”. Sango stated, a hint of fear laced in his voice as the thunder boomed in agreement.

“Yes, but didn’t we agree to end her existence” “But not this way” Another voice spoke from the table “We just awoken what we assumed we would kill” murmurs followed as Yemoja spoke. Her voice as smooth as the sea yet so strong. Fear surrounded the room. “We should end her now” the minor gods chanted “there is still time before she awakens” “Silence!” Sango’s voice raged through the golden space and swept at the clouds. “Now is not the time to fret” at least that was what he told the gods, the turmoil in his head said otherwise All episodes of this story can be found here >> https://www.ebonystory.com/story/eve.

It rang panic ‘you will no longer be king, she is coming back’. Still drenched, she began to curse the world for such cruelty, she could feel something crawl out of her being, it crawled against her insides as she screamed, 'maybe death was finally coming for her' she thought and closed her eyes, slowly accepting her fate. "My dear girl" a voice spoke in her ears. It was hypnotic, it urged her to listen and she did so in a daze. "Accept me and curse the world, bring everything you hate to its demise let them kneel before you" taken by grief, she cursed the world, cursed those that betrayed her and did the unthinkable, cursed the gods and in that moment the words fell out her lips, pure darkness was born and the innocent soul was no more.

She was dead but what took over was very much alive. For after all she had lived life longer than any other. She smiled and got up from the earth, effortlessly seducing all that was nature until a bolt of lightning struck, and just as effortlessly she held the bolt directed to her heart and asked looking up to the sky. “Sango my boy, mummy is back” she sang and the whole of the heavens broke loose. ******* Yay.
