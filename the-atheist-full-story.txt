### **Full Story: The Atheist**

The narrative centers around Josh, whose life is shattered when his parents are brutally murdered by a menacing clan named 'FACELESS.' He vows to achieve vengeance against every member of this brutal group, yet recognizes that he cannot accomplish the mission alone; companionship is essential. Will Josh succeed in his quest for revenge, or will the experienced hands of the vicious clan turn him into another victim? This action-packed thriller invites readers to embark on a "blissful and pleasant ride."

### **Summary**

"The Atheist" chronicles the vengeful journey of Josh following the brutal murder of his parents by the 'FACELESS' clan. Aided by Jude, Josh embarks on a mission across various episodes—marked by combat, stealth infiltration, and intense confrontation—to eliminate the assassins responsible for his family's demise.
