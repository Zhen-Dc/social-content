# Stolen Innocence - Shotlist Continuation Part 001

## p001-s001 - Elder at the fence
Beat: Amara sees Elder Okoro at the fence after leaving the kitchen.
Camera: 50mm medium shot from Amara's side, Elder framed across the fence, tense quiet afternoon
Performance: Amara pauses with hungry uncertainty; Elder leans calmly on the fence.

## p001-s002 - Greeting
Beat: "Amara, how are you?" / "Good afternoon, sir. I am fine."
Camera: 50mm dialogue two-shot, fence line separating them
Performance: Elder's smile is gentle but watchful; Amara keeps her shoulders small.

## p001-s003 - Chicken pox noticed
Beat: Elder notices the chicken-pox marks while speaking to Amara.
Camera: tight 85mm over-the-shoulder from behind Amara's pox-marked cheek and shoulder; Elder Okoro's speaking face fills the right half of the frame at the fence
Performance: Elder Okoro speaks with his lower jaw dropped mid-word while Amara holds still in the foreground.

## p001-s004 - Alone question
Beat: "So you are alone?"
Camera: strict left-facing side-profile 85mm head-and-shoulders close-up of Elder Okoro at the fence, Amara is off-camera
Performance: Elder Okoro asks the question with his mouth wide open in an oval oh-shape, eyes aimed left toward Amara.

## p001-s005 - Amara admits hunger
Beat: "Koko ate my food."
Camera: three-quarter close-up of Amara looking down, kitchen doorway behind her
Performance: Her lips press together before she admits it.

## p001-s006 - Elder invites her
Beat: "Come. Do not worry. I have food in my kitchen."
Camera: low 50mm shot along side path to Elder's house
Performance: Elder gestures toward his compound; Amara stays at the threshold.

## p001-s007 - Enamel bowl
Beat: Elder returns with warm food in an enamel bowl.
Camera: insert shot of old hands holding enamel bowl, steam rising
Performance: His hands are steady and deliberate.

## p001-s008 - Amara eats
Beat: Amara sits near his doorway and eats.
Camera: 50mm candid side shot, bowl in her lap
Performance: She eats quickly, still weak, unaware she is being watched.

## p001-s009 - Elder watches
Beat: Elder watches her with a calm smile.
Camera: 85mm side-profile close-up on Elder
Performance: Slow blink, small controlled smile, eyes fixed off-camera at Amara.

## p001-s010 - Somto returns
Beat: Somto rushes back home, trying to hide that she went out.
Camera: 35mm action shot at doorway
Performance: Somto slips into the compound, wrapper loose, breath fast.

## p001-s011 - Somto does not care
Beat: Amara tells Somto about the dog and Elder's food.
Camera: side-by-side interior two-shot
Performance: Amara explains; Somto looks toward the kitchen instead of comforting her.

## p001-s012 - Evening cooking
Beat: Somto rushes to cook before the parents return.
Camera: kitchen action shot, smoky practical light
Performance: Somto stirs food fast, glancing toward the compound entrance.

## p001-s013 - Parents return
Beat: The parents return from the farm smelling of soil and maize leaves.
Camera: over-the-shoulder from behind Amara toward Mother and Father entering with farm tools
Performance: Mother bends toward Amara; father drops his cutlass by the door; Amara stands small at the doorway edge.

## p001-s014 - Amara lies for Somto
Beat: "She was sleeping. I did not want to wake her."
Camera: over-the-shoulder from behind Father toward Amara in the foreground, Somto blurred in the far corner
Performance: Amara avoids Somto's eyes; Somto's shoulders loosen with relief.

## p001-s015 - Mother will thank Elder
Beat: "Tomorrow I will go and thank him."
Camera: 50mm close-up on Mother tying headscarf tighter
Performance: Mother sighs from exhaustion and gratitude.

## p001-s016 - Night sky
Beat: That night, the moon hangs above the village.
Camera: 35mm establishing shot, full or half moon above thatched roofs
Performance: Clouds drift; village is quiet and ominous.

## p001-s017 - Dream circle
Beat: Amara stands in the middle of black-gowned people.
Camera: wide night shot, Amara small in center of circle
Performance: She turns in panic, searching for an exit.

## p001-s018 - Finds Elder
Beat: She sees Elder Okoro among them.
Camera: 85mm over-the-shoulder from behind Amara through two soft black-gowned shoulders toward Elder Okoro in the midground
Performance: Her breathing catches; Elder remains still, separated from the group by a thin strip of moonlight.

## p001-s019 - Take me home
Beat: "Sir, please take me home. I want to go home."
Camera: 50mm handheld dialogue shot, Amara reaching toward Elder
Performance: Her eyes widen, mouth trembles, hand reaches and stops short.

## p001-s020 - Special people
Beat: "You are among special people now."
Camera: side-profile close-up of Elder speaking at night
Performance: Elder bends slightly, voice calm, eyes fixed on her.

## p001-s021 - Poverty catches her
Beat: The promise of helping her poor family makes Amara stop crying.
Camera: tight close-up on Amara, moonlight, black silhouettes behind
Performance: Tears pause; her brows soften with dangerous hope.

## p001-s022 - Wish request
Beat: "Can you make my father rich?"
Camera: 85mm close-up, Amara looking up toward Elder not camera
Performance: She swallows, voice small, fingers gripping dress fabric.

## p001-s023 - Calabash POV
Beat: Elder hands the calabash toward her.
Camera: front-view low-angle POV shot from Amara's eye line; Elder extends calabash toward camera
Performance: His arms stretch forward; calabash fills foreground.

## p001-s024 - Calabash insert
Beat: The calabash is smooth, dark, tied with red thread.
Camera: 45mm macro insert, focus locked on calabash and red thread
Performance: Object still, background fully blurred.

## p001-s025 - Instruction
Beat: "Hide it under your parents bamboo bed."
Camera: side-profile close-up of Elder holding calabash low
Performance: His mouth forms the words slowly; his face is controlled.

## p001-s026 - Warning
Beat: "If you tell anyone, your father will die."
Camera: 85mm tight close-up on Elder, moonlight shadow across face
Performance: His smile disappears; jaw tightens, eyes harden.

## p001-s027 - Promise
Beat: "I will not tell."
Camera: close-up on Amara clutching calabash
Performance: Hands shake; lips barely move; shoulders curl inward.

## p001-s028 - Wake up
Beat: Amara wakes beside Somto.
Camera: dim bedroom, Somto asleep on mat, Amara jolting awake
Performance: Amara lifts her head sharply, breath fast.

## p001-s029 - Real calabash
Beat: Amara sees the same calabash after waking.
Camera: under-the-bed POV shot from floor level beneath the bamboo bed, calabash large in the foreground, Amara's shocked face visible beyond it
Performance: Amara looks under the bed in shock at the calabash.

## p001-s030 - Tiptoe
Beat: Amara tiptoes out while Somto sleeps.
Camera: low angle from floor, Amara carrying calabash, Somto asleep behind
Performance: Amara moves carefully, toes placed silently.

## p001-s031 - Parents room
Beat: She enters her parents' dim room.
Camera: over-the-shoulder from behind Amara toward bamboo bed
Performance: She freezes at father's breathing.

## p001-s032 - Under bed
Beat: She pushes the calabash under the bamboo bed.
Camera: low floor-level shot, hand pushing calabash under bamboo bed
Performance: Her hand trembles; bed legs loom above.

## p001-s033 - Morning excitement
Beat: By morning, fear turns into excitement.
Camera: warm morning close-up, Amara looking at father off-camera
Performance: Her smile is small and secret, eyes bright.

## p001-s034 - Thank you visit
Beat: Mother takes Amara to thank Elder Okoro.
Camera: over-the-shoulder from behind Mother at Elder's compound doorway; Elder faces her in the doorway, Amara half-hidden beside Mother's wrapper
Performance: Mother speaks warmly with one hand lifted; Amara lowers her eyes and grips the cloth at her side.

## p001-s035 - Like my own child
Beat: "She is like my own child."
Camera: over-the-shoulder from behind Mother toward Elder and Amara, Elder in sharp focus, Amara small at frame edge
Performance: Elder's eyes land on Amara as he says it; Amara's mouth tightens without looking up.

## p001-s036 - Final dread
Beat: Only Amara knows the calabash is under the bed.
Camera: side-profile close-up of Amara walking away from Elder's compound
Performance: Her pride flickers into unease; she looks back once.
