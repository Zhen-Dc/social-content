# Asset Plan

## Characters

- Nara: West African woman, late 20s to early 30s, deep warm brown skin, wet natural coiled hair with partial scalp braids, indigo Yoruba-style wrapper dress, dark red sash, muddy and rain-soaked throughout.
- Kade: West African thunder king, mid 40s, storm-blue agbada with bronze embroidery, broad muscular build, gray-streaked beard, controlled fear.
- Marea: West African water goddess, late 30s, sea-green iro and buba, long wet braids with cowries, urgent restraint.

## Locations

- Storm-Flooded Earth: nighttime sacred grove, black mud, wet roots, rain, storm clouds, natural lightning, no modern structures.
- Cloud Realm: bronze-pillared divine chamber above clouds, carved stone, golden mist, thunderlight, no readable text.

## Props And Effects

- Mud contact: Nara's cheek, hands, knees, clothing, and hair must stay physically in contact with wet mud in early scenes.
- Darkness: smoky black presence near Nara's ear and hand, organic and subtle, not a separate monster.
- Lightning: natural branching lightning, later held in Nara's palm, no superhero glow suit.
- Nature response: wet grass, roots, and leaves leaning toward Nara.

## Generation Rules

- One sentence equals one image.
- No text inside generated images.
- Still image only; motion comes later from Ken Burns in HyperFrames.
- Captions are added only in the video timeline and must match the narration exactly.
- Use natural ungraded camera color. Do not add LUT-style color grading.
