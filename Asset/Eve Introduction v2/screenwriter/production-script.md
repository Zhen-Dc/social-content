# Eve Introduction v2 Production Script

## Screenwriter Pass

Controlling idea: betrayal becomes apocalypse when a broken woman accepts power instead of mercy.

Value movement: helplessness becomes command; death becomes return; grief becomes a weapon.

Format: first-person POV narration, one narration sentence per clip, visible action written as production beats.

### Scene 001

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA lies face-down in black mud. Rain beats her hair flat. Her fingers twitch once.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
I used to think betrayal was just a wound.

### Scene 002

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA'S cheek sinks deeper into the mud. Her eyelids flutter without focus.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Then I died inside it.

### Scene 003

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Rainwater crawls around NARA'S shoulders. The sky presses low over her body.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
The rain pressed me into the mud while the sky folded low above my body.

### Scene 004

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Thunder cracks overhead. NARA'S ribs rise under her soaked wrapper.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Thunder answered every breath I lost.

### Scene 005

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA grips mud in her fist. Her jaw tightens. Rain washes dirt across her lips.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
I could still feel the people who broke me, their lies moving through my chest like knives.

### Scene 006

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA'S hand stops shaking. Her fingers open and press into the earth.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
So I stopped begging to survive.

### Scene 007

**EXT. CLOUD REALM - NIGHT**

Above the storm, carved bronze pillars stand inside a cloud chamber. Ancient figures turn toward the earth below.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Somewhere above the clouds, the old ones panicked.

### Scene 008

**EXT. CLOUD REALM - NIGHT**

KADE stands at the center of the chamber. Thunderlight pulses across his face.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Kade, king of thunder, knew what my grief had opened.

### Scene 009

**EXT. CLOUD REALM - NIGHT**

MAREA steps from blue mist. Her wet braids cling to her cheek as she points toward the storm below.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Marea warned them there was still time to end me before I woke.

### Scene 010

**EXT. CLOUD REALM - NIGHT**

The gods stare downward. The cloud floor flashes with a pulse from the earth.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
But they were too late.

### Scene 011

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Smoke curls beside NARA'S ear. Her eyes open a fraction.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
A voice slipped into my ear, soft as smoke and colder than death.

### Scene 012

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Darkness gathers under NARA'S palm. The mud around her hand ripples outward.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
It told me to accept the darkness.

### Scene 013

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA'S mouth moves without sound. Rain beads on her cracked lower lip.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
It told me to curse the world that made me kneel.

### Scene 014

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA'S eyes lock open. Her breath stops.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
So I did.

### Scene 015

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA pushes one elbow into the mud. A faint red glow moves under her skin.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
I cursed the betrayers.

### Scene 016

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA turns her face upward. Rainwater runs down her throat.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
I cursed the gods.

### Scene 017

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Her hand closes around nothing. The mud blackens beneath her fingers.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
I cursed the life that had left me empty.

### Scene 018

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

The earth under NARA'S body swells like it is breathing.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
And something ancient answered.

### Scene 019

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA'S face goes still. Her old expression disappears.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
The girl in the mud died that night.

### Scene 020

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA rises to her knees. Mud drops from her face in heavy strings.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
But I stood up wearing her face.

### Scene 021

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Grass, roots, and wet leaves lean toward NARA'S body.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Nature bent toward me.

### Scene 022

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Lightning drops through the clouds toward NARA'S chest.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Lightning fell from heaven, aimed straight at my heart.

### Scene 023

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA raises one hand. Lightning stops inside her palm.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
I caught it in my hand.

### Scene 024

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA turns into the rain. A smile builds slowly on her mud-covered face.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Then I looked into the storm and smiled.

### Scene 025

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

Far above, KADE stands frozen in thunderlight. NARA'S smile remains below.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Kade, my boy, I whispered.

### Scene 026

**EXT. STORM-FLOODED EARTH - NIGHT**

NARA stands upright in the storm. Lightning burns behind her open hand.

**NARA (V.O.)**  
Mother is back.
