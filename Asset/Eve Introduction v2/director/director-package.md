# Director Package: Eve Introduction v2

## Director's Interpretation

This is a first-person mythic awakening. The audience should feel a woman move from betrayal and bodily collapse into supernatural authority. The camera follows Nara's psychology: low, close, and trapped in mud at first; then gradually more upright, wider, and calmer as she accepts the ancient force.

## Visual Strategy

- Genre/texture: ultra-realistic West African mythic drama, rain, mud, practical lightning, natural ungraded camera color.
- Focal character: Nara, the narrator and returned mother-force.
- Camera principle: still-image coverage with Ken Burns later; every still must feel like a frame pulled from a film.
- Lighting principle: storm lightning, cloud glow, rain reflection; no studio fill and no color grade.
- Sound principle: female narration only for this pass; no SFX or background music.
- Final image: Nara upright in the storm, hand holding lightning, calm enough to say "Mother is back."

## Blocking

Nara scenes keep one primary axis: mud/earth below, storm/heaven above. Early frames place Nara low in the lower third, physically pressed into mud. Midpoint frames move her hands and face into the center. Final frames place her upright, centered or low-angle, with the storm behind her.

Cloud realm scenes keep Kade and Marea above the storm. They face downward toward Nara's location; they do not occupy the same physical space as Nara.

## Performance Direction

Nara's performance arc:
- Scenes 001-006: collapsed breath, slack jaw, unfocused eyes, fingers barely responding.
- Scenes 011-018: jaw and hand tension increase; fear becomes decision.
- Scenes 019-020: old expression dies; shoulders square.
- Scenes 021-026: calm authority, relaxed shoulders, lifted chin, controlled smile.

Kade's performance: restrained fear; jaw clenched, nostrils flare, eyes fixed downward.

Marea's performance: urgent restraint; raised hand, pinned brows, lips parted.
