Prologue.

A woman lies in mud beneath a violent sky. Thunder answers her grief while rain covers her body. In what she believes are her final moments, she thinks only of betrayal and decides there is nothing left to live for.

The gods watch from a golden space. They argue over whether they have ended her existence or awakened something worse. Sango fears that the woman's grief has released a force that will disrupt the world. Yemoja and the minor gods debate killing her before she awakens.

Still drenched in rain, the woman curses the world, the people who betrayed her, and the gods themselves. A strange voice enters her ear, asking her to accept its power and make the world kneel.

In her grief, she accepts. Darkness is born. The innocent soul dies, but something ancient takes over her body.

She rises from the earth with unnatural power. Nature bends toward her. When lightning strikes, she catches it effortlessly, looks toward the sky, and announces that she has returned. Heaven breaks loose.
