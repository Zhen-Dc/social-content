## Scene Script

### Scene 001 - The Dream At The Window

Zahra studies at night beside an open window. She dreams of becoming a doctor and frames education as the one place where her future still feels possible.

Visual: `images/scene-001.png`

### Scene 002 - The Call

Her father's voice interrupts the peace. Zahra freezes before leaving her desk, sensing that the ordinary night is about to become something else.

Visual: `images/scene-002.png`

### Scene 003 - The Room Feels Wrong

Zahra enters her parents' room. Her father is grave; her mother avoids her eyes. The silence shows that a decision has already been made.

Visual: `images/scene-003.png`

### Scene 004 - The Revelation

Her father tells her about the family promise. Zahra's shock is not loud at first; it shows in her breath, her eyes, and the way the room seems to close around her.

Visual: `images/scene-004.png`

### Scene 005 - The Plea

Zahra turns to her mother for rescue, but her mother cannot undo the decision. This is the emotional collapse of the scene.

Visual: `images/scene-005.png`

### Scene 006 - The Resolve

Back in her room, Zahra cries beside her open book. By morning, grief has shifted into a private decision: if nobody will fight for her future, she will learn to fight for it herself.

Visual: `images/scene-006.png`

## Performance Notes

The narrator should sound intimate, wounded, and controlled. Avoid theatrical crying. The final lines should carry quiet determination rather than triumph.
