I used to think betrayal was just a wound.

Then I died inside it.

The rain pressed me into the mud while the sky folded low above my body. Thunder answered every breath I lost. I could still feel the people who broke me, their lies moving through my chest like knives.

So I stopped begging to survive.

Somewhere above the clouds, the old ones panicked. Kade, king of thunder, knew what my grief had opened. Marea warned them there was still time to end me before I woke.

But they were too late.

A voice slipped into my ear, soft as smoke and colder than death. It told me to accept the darkness. It told me to curse the world that made me kneel.

So I did.

I cursed the betrayers. I cursed the gods. I cursed the life that had left me empty.

And something ancient answered.

The girl in the mud died that night. But I stood up wearing her face.

Nature bent toward me. Lightning fell from heaven, aimed straight at my heart.

I caught it in my hand.

Then I looked into the storm and smiled.

Kade, my boy, I whispered.

Mother is back.
