# Director Package - Eve Introduction

## Director's Interpretation

This is a rebirth scene, not a death scene. The audience should feel grief turn into dread as Nara stops being a victim and becomes an ancient force.

## Visual Strategy

- Genre/texture: dark mythic African fantasy, rain, mud, lightning, golden cloud chamber.
- Focal character: Nara.
- Camera principle: start low and intimate, then become mythic and upward-looking.
- Lighting principle: cold storm blues against divine gold and violet darkness.
- Sound principle: rain and thunder carry the first half; silence and lightning carry the final reveal.
- Final image: Nara catches lightning and smiles into the sky.

## Blocking

Nara begins low in foreground mud. The gods occupy elevated space above the clouds. By the final scene, Nara owns the center frame and the sky becomes the threatened space.

## Performance Direction

Nara's grief is restrained: wet lower eyelids, tight mouth, shallow breath, shoulders held still. The turn begins in her hand gripping mud, then her chin lifts and her eyes settle. Kade's fear is hidden in his jaw and stillness.

## Shot Plan

| Shot | Duration | Lens/Camera | Action | Performance | Continuity |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10.5s | low 35mm | Nara lies in mud | exhausted betrayal | burgundy dress, wet hair, scar |

| 3 | 13s | wide 28mm | gods argue above | Kade hides fear | gold chamber, storm below |
| 4 | 14s | medium 50mm | smoke whispers | grief becomes focus | black smoke at ear |
| 5 | 10s | low 35mm | Nara rises | ancient confidence | nature bends inward |

## QA

Identity, costume, mud, rain, scar, and dark burgundy dress must remain consistent across all Nara scenes.
