Summary:
Williams wakes into terror after being kidnapped on a journey to Lagos. He is trapped in a dark room with other captives, surrounded by armed men led by Capoul. The captors make it clear that the prisoners are not being held for ransom; they are intended to be killed and sold for body parts.

Fear almost overwhelms Williams, but he remembers his mother's advice about praying and using every chance to survive. He begins speaking with another captive, Ademola, and slowly builds the courage to propose an escape. The captives use the little they have in the room to reach the roof, slip into the backyard, and wait for the right moment while the kidnappers patrol outside.

When the guards move away, Williams and the others finally jump the fence and flee into the bush. The story is driven by panic, faith, quick thinking, and the thin difference between freezing in fear and taking the only chance available.
