Summary:
Raymond, a thirteen-year-old boy, decides to experiment with drugs while his mother is away and his older sister is out. He wants to feel grown and rebellious, copying what he learned from a schoolmate. His plan collapses when his mother comes home unexpectedly and catches him.

Instead of reacting with the beating or shouting Raymond expects, his mother takes him out into the nightlife he thinks he is ready for. At a club, Raymond watches her move through a dangerous adult world and begins to feel the shame, fear, and helplessness she must feel when she watches him make destructive choices. She eventually reveals that the work she does at night is how she supports his and his sister's education.

The lesson becomes even darker when she identifies a poisoned drink and explains that trust can be deadly in the streets. Raymond's fantasy of being grown is replaced by a clearer understanding of danger, sacrifice, and the hidden cost of his mother's survival.
