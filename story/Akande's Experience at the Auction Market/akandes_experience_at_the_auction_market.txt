**Akande's Experience at the Auction Market**
***
“I hate this, I don’t want to be here.” That was all Akande was thinking from the moment his father dragged him down to this place. He had not been a great fan of the new system of slavery... “Nibo ni iw? nl?” (Where are you going?) he swallowed hard at the calm yet scary look on his father’s face. “Ko si ibi ti” (Nowhere) he stammered. "You should buy one for yourself, you need it here" Akande pleaded with his mother's warning...
